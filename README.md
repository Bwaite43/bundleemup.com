# Bundle 'Em Up Hendricks County

**Website: [bundleuphendrickscounty.org](https://bundleuphendrickscounty.org/)**

Bundle 'Em Up is a community winter coat drive and free coat giveaway in Hendricks County, Indiana. Each fall, neighbors donate gently used winter coats at drop-off sites across the county. Then, on giveaway day at the Hendricks County Fairgrounds in Danville, anyone who needs a coat can come and pick one out for free.

## Who it helps

Winter in central Indiana is cold, and a warm coat is one of the most basic things a family can go without. Bundle 'Em Up is for anyone in Hendricks County who needs a coat: kids heading to school, parents, seniors, and neighbors going through a hard stretch. There is no sign-up, no paperwork, and no questions asked. Everyone is welcome.

Giveaway day is built for families. Kids are invited to wear a costume and enjoy indoor trick-or-treating and refreshments while their families pick out coats. Partner organizations are also on hand with information on the Energy Assistance Program, and IU Health provides free blood pressure screenings.

## How it works

1. **Coats are collected** at drop-off sites in Plainfield, Danville, Avon, Brownsburg, and Clayton, including town halls, churches, libraries, and community organizations. Clean, gently used coats in any size are welcome.
2. **Coats are given away** on a Saturday in October at the fairgrounds. Come, browse, and take what you need.

Current dates, times, and the full list of drop-off sites are always on the [website](https://bundleuphendrickscounty.org/).

## How you can help

- **Donate a coat.** Drop a gently used winter coat at any collection site before the deadline.
- **Spread the word.** Share the website or the printable flyer with your school, church, workplace, or neighbors.
- **Come if you need a coat.** Bring the whole family.
